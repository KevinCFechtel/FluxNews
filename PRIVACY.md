# Privacy Policy

FluxNews is a free and open-source client for a Miniflux server you configure.
FluxNews does not include its own advertising, analytics, device-tracking, or
automatic anonymous bug-reporting service.

## Connections and data

To function, the app connects to **your configured Miniflux server** using the
server URL and API key you provide. Those credentials are stored in the app's
platform-protected local account storage and are used to authenticate to that
server; FluxNews does not send your Miniflux API key to article publishers or
advertising/analytics services.

The app may also make **direct network requests to third-party content hosts**
when fetching article images, feed artwork, or podcast/media content, including
downloads and streaming. Those hosts receive the ordinary request information
needed to serve the content (for example your IP address and requested URL).
The hosts' own privacy practices may apply. These content requests are part of
the reader's functionality; they are not FluxNews tracking or telemetry.

An explicitly initiated configuration backup or export sends data only to the
destination you choose through the platform's sharing or storage facilities.
Protect exported configuration files according to the options provided by the
app and the selected destination.

## Diagnostic information

FluxNews does not automatically upload anonymous bug reports or usage analytics.
On-device diagnostics are intended for local troubleshooting; any logs or reports
you explicitly share are handled by the destination or recipient you choose.

FluxNews is developed as a free and open-source project, not as a personal-data
collection service.
